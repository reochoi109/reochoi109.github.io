## Starting point

Korean learning material divided only into grammar and vocabulary can make it difficult to encounter everyday life and culture in context. Molru starts with something worth reading, while keeping learning support nearby for the moments when it is needed.

## Content structure

Content is organized into Daily Korean, K-Culture, Stories, and Korean Now. The topics cover everyday conversations, customs and culture, personal stories, and current expressions and trends so that language and context can be understood together.

Topic, level, and estimated reading time help learners choose an article suited to them rather than browsing an undifferentiated list.

## Learning experience

- Listen to natural Korean while following the article
- Reveal translations only when help is needed
- Understand vocabulary and expressions inside sentences and stories
- Encounter contemporary Korean life and culture alongside the language

## Building and operating it

I designed the site structure, topic taxonomy, article template, and learning features as one reading journey. The content connections also consider learners who arrive directly on an article through search, helping them continue to related topics and stories.
