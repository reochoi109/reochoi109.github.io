Background

Theater→Backend engineering

I started theater to become less reserved. Standing in front of others was difficult at first, but I kept performing and addressing my weaknesses over roughly ten years. At university, I studied theater and film and worked in acting, lighting, and stage production.

Theater taught me that steady learning and practice can lead to change, even when the starting point is unfamiliar. That experience supported me as I began learning software development without a computer science background.

After completing a backend development program in 2022, I joined Laon Road as an intern. I investigated problems through research and asked colleagues for help when needed. I later converted to a full-time role, taking on production maintenance and the design and development of a new relay server.
