Slow Mind

Free meditation and focus app

A lightweight meditation app with six natural environments, ambient sound, and focus timers. It works immediately without an account or installation.

Why I built it

I wanted a small web tool that could be used immediately for a quiet pause or focused session, without an account or complicated setup. Rather than presenting a large library of meditation content, the product centers on choosing an environment and staying with it.

User experience

Users can choose Forest, Ocean, Space, Mountain, Rain, or Sky and play matching visuals and ambient sound. The controls are deliberately limited to play, pause, mute, and volume.

Focus Mode adds 5, 10, 15, and 30-minute timers plus an open-ended session. This allows the same product to support meditation, calm breathing, and quiet work.

Product principles

  • Start without an account or installation
  • Keep the screen and sound from competing for attention
  • Make the core controls understandable without instructions
  • Support both short pauses and longer focus sessions

Wireframes

The public pages and the core meditation and focus-player states were designed as complete page flows.

What I continue to evaluate

The core value is reducing steps rather than adding information. Slow Mind remains publicly available for free, and I continue to assess the experience by whether a user can begin at the moment they need it.
